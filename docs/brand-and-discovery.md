# Loop — brand and discovery draft

This is proposed marketing and metadata for review. It does not represent a published website, marketing campaign or App Store listing.

## Brand structure

| Use | Name or copy |
|---|---|
| In-app and home-screen name | Loop |
| First marketing mention and website brand | Loop Tech Support |
| App Store name | Loop: Tech Support |
| App Store subtitle | Simple device troubleshooting |
| Tagline | Tech support, one tap at a time. |
| Subscription | Full Access |

Loop is short, easy to say and suited to the app's tap-by-tap experience. “Tech Support” supplies the purpose when someone first encounters the brand. Use the fuller name consistently in external marketing, then shorten it to Loop after that first mention. Explain the experience as guided self-help so customers know what they are getting.

The name has existing competition. Microsoft already uses Loop for its collaboration app, including on iOS and Android. The descriptor helps communicate a different use, but this research does not establish name availability, trademark clearance or a right to use the name. Confirm the intended App Store name, domain and relevant brand rights before public launch or a marketing spend.

[Microsoft's official Loop overview](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/loop/get-started-with-microsoft-loop)

## Website metadata

**Page title:** Loop Tech Support | Simple Device Troubleshooting

**Meta description:** Get help with everyday tech. Loop guides you through simple checks for phones, TVs, computers, printers and apps. Save your devices in My Tech.

**Main heading:** Tech support, one tap at a time.

**Supporting copy:** Choose your device, tell Loop what's going wrong and follow simple steps to work out what to try next. Keep your devices together in My Tech for an easier start next time.

**Primary action:** Start

For web discovery, write useful pages around supported problems such as a printer not connecting or a device losing Wi-Fi. Each page should explain its actual coverage and link into the matching app experience. Avoid listing unsupported devices or adding vendor names solely to attract searches. Website search performance and App Store search performance need separate measurement.

## Short marketing copy

**Introduction:** Meet Loop Tech Support. Simple, guided help for everyday technology problems.

**Benefit copy:** Printer not connecting? Wi-Fi playing up? Start with a few taps, follow clear checks and keep track of what you've tried.

**Full Access copy:** Go further with the advanced guides available for supported devices and apps. Explore the included coverage before you subscribe.

**Capability wording:** Loop offers guided self-help. It does not remotely control or scan other devices, provide live technicians, or generate live AI diagnoses. Avoid promises that it fixes every device or problem. Core navigation has 40 language packs; guide content is currently in English. Some upcoming catalogue entries are identification information only.

## App Store discovery

The proposed name is **18 characters** and subtitle **29 characters**. Both fit Apple's 30-character limits. The `app-store-listing.md` keyword field is **100 characters** and avoids repeating title and subtitle terms. Keywords are a starting hypothesis based on supported use cases, not a measured demand or ranking forecast.

Apple describes text relevance and user behaviour as search factors. Use the descriptor to establish purpose, show the actual experience in screenshots, and review search impressions and conversion after launch. Localize listing text and screenshots for the markets being served, while stating accurately which parts of the app are translated.

## Release details still to complete

The legal operator, support email, support/privacy/terms URLs, final domain, name availability, native device validation and purchase test results remain release inputs.
