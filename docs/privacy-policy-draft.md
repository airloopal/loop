## Information on your device

The app saves your My Tech device choices and nicknames, language preference and troubleshooting progress on your device so you can continue later. The prepared version does not send this local information to an operator server and does not include advertising or analytics services. Apple device backups may retain app data according to your device settings.

You can remove locally saved app information using the app's Erase local data action. Deleting local data does not cancel an App Store subscription or delete Apple's transaction records. If you restore a device backup, backed-up information may return.

## Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. Loop uses Apple's StoreKit transaction information to determine whether Full Access is active. The app does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple handles its payment and account information under its own privacy terms.

## Sharing and support

When you choose to share a troubleshooting summary, iOS shows its share controls and sends the selected text through the destination you choose. Check the summary before sharing it. Nothing is sent to the operator simply because a guide is opened.

If you email support, the operator receives your email address and anything you include.
