## Promotional text

Tech trouble? Start with a few simple taps. Save your Apple devices in My Tech and follow clear checks to work out what to try next.

## Description

Loop helps you work through everyday problems with Apple devices and software, one clear step at a time.

Choose your device, describe the problem, and follow a guided pathway. Start with iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch, AirPods or iCloud. Loop helps you narrow down an issue without needing technical vocabulary.

Save your devices in My Tech for a shortcut next time. Follow the available basic guides for free. Loop provides troubleshooting guidance; it does not supply replacement parts, book repairs or perform physical repairs.

Full Access is an optional annual subscription. The current advanced library covers iPhone Wi-Fi and Mac Wi-Fi troubleshooting, with progress tracking and a shareable support summary. Preview the included coverage before subscribing. A device appearing in the catalogue does not mean every problem or model-specific procedure is covered. Some devices only offer general checks or links to official support.

Core navigation includes 40 language options. Troubleshooting guides and some newer interface text are currently in English. Bundled guides can be read offline; purchases and external support pages require an internet connection.

The current local subscription price and renewal terms appear before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through your Apple account settings. Manage or restore purchases from within the app.

Loop is an independent service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple. Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
