## Promotional text

Tech trouble? Start with a few simple taps. Save your devices in My Tech and follow clear checks to work out what to try next.

## Description

Loop helps you work through everyday technology problems, one clear step at a time.

Choose a device or app, describe the problem, and follow a guided pathway. Whether you are checking a printer connection, setting up a streaming app or finding why your Wi-Fi is not working, Loop helps you narrow down the issue without needing technical vocabulary.

Save the technology you own in My Tech so it is easier to find next time. Follow basic troubleshooting guides for free, keep track of your progress, and share a summary of the checks you have tried when you need more help.

Full Access is an optional annual subscription for the advanced guides available for supported devices and apps. You can see the included coverage before subscribing. A device appearing in the catalogue does not mean every problem or model-specific procedure is covered. New and upcoming devices may be listed for identification before troubleshooting is available.

Core navigation is available in 40 languages. Troubleshooting guide text is currently in English. Bundled guides can be read without an internet connection; purchases and external manufacturer support pages require connectivity.

The current local subscription price and renewal terms appear before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through your Apple account settings. You can manage or restore purchases from within the app.

Loop is an independent service. Product names and trademarks identify compatible technology and belong to their respective owners; their use does not imply endorsement or affiliation.
